STR 301 — Competitive Strategy

Quarter 3 · Year 1 · 10 Weeks · Dec 7, 2026 – Feb 12, 2027

STR 301

Competitive Strategy

Upcoming — Q3 Year 1
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Strategy is the discipline that asks: why do some companies win consistently while others with more resources fail? Okta is a perfect strategy case study — it entered a market dominated by Microsoft and Oracle and outmaneuvered both by focusing relentlessly on independence and the identity layer. Understanding WHY that worked — using Porter's Five Forces, competitive positioning, and platform network effects — will change how you see every business decision you make. This is the course that turns engineers into strategists.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply Porter's Five Forces to analyze industry attractiveness and competitive dynamics
  • Build competitive advantage through cost leadership, differentiation, or focus strategies
  • Analyze technology disruption using the Christensen framework
  • Design platform and ecosystem strategy using network effects
  • Develop a full strategic plan for a business unit or new venture

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
What Is Strategy? — Rumelt's Good Strategy Framework
Open →
Topics:
  • The difference between goals and strategy
  • Rumelt's "kernel" model: diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions
  • Why most "strategic plans" are just goals in disguise
  • Common strategy failures: fluff, dog-eat-dog, template-filling
  • Apply to: what IS Okta's actual strategy?
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1
  • Identify Okta's strategy kernel: write its diagnosis, guiding policy, and 3 coherent actions
Week 2
Industry Analysis — Porter's Five Forces
Topics:
  • Five Forces framework (threat of entry, substitutes, buyer/supplier power, rivalry)
  • Industry profitability analysis
  • Applying Five Forces to identity security market
  • How Zero Trust trend reshapes the industry structure
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 2
  • Five Forces analysis of the identity security market
  • Reflection
Week 3
Competitive Advantage — Cost Leadership, Differentiation & Focus
Topics:
  • Cost leadership vs differentiation trade-offs
  • Stuck in the middle trap
  • Okta's differentiation strategy vs Microsoft's cost bundling
  • Operational effectiveness vs strategic positioning
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 3
  • Competitive positioning analysis for Okta vs Microsoft Entra
  • Reflection
Week 4
Value Chain Analysis
Topics:
  • Primary and support activities in the value chain
  • Sources of cost advantage along the chain
  • Linkages between activities
  • How Okta's integration marketplace creates value chain differentiation
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 4
  • Value chain analysis for a SaaS company of your choice
  • Reflection
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
Disruptive Innovation — The Christensen Framework
Topics:
  • Christensen's disruptive innovation theory
  • Low-end vs new-market disruption
  • How Okta disrupted on-premise IAM (Ping/RSA)
  • Is Okta itself vulnerable to disruption from Microsoft bundling?
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 5
  • Mid-course: Disruption analysis for the identity security market
  • Reflection on Okta's incumbent risk
Week 6
Platform & Ecosystem Strategy
Topics:
  • Platform vs pipeline business models
  • Multi-sided markets and network effects
  • Ecosystems and complementors strategy
  • Okta's Integration Network as platform strategy
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 6
  • Platform strategy analysis for a company of your choice
  • Reflection
Week 7
Blue Ocean Strategy — Creating New Market Space
Topics:
  • Blue Ocean Strategy framework (value innovation)
  • Eliminate/Reduce/Raise/Create grid

  • Cirque du Soleil and how it applies to tech
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 7
  • Blue Ocean canvas for your startup idea
  • Reflection
Week 8
Corporate Strategy — Diversification, M&A & Portfolio
Topics:

Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 8
  • BCG matrix for a diversified tech company's product portfolio
  • Reflection
Week 9
Strategy in Tech — Network Effects & Winner-Take-All
Topics:
  • Network effects (direct/indirect/local/global)
  • Winner-take-all dynamics in tech platforms
  • Data network effects and AI moats
  • How Okta's identity data creates a compounding advantage
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 9
  • Network effects analysis for Okta's developer ecosystem
  • Reflection
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Full Competitive Strategy Analysis
Topics:
  • Full competitive strategy analysis — choose a company, apply Five Forces + value chain + disruption + network effects
  • Write a complete strategic assessment and 3-year strategic plan
Deliverables:
  • Final: Full competitive strategy analysis (due date)
  • Strategic plan document
  • Executive briefing (2 pages)
