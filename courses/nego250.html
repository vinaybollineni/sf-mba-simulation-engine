NEGO 250 — Negotiation & Conflict Resolution

Quarter 3 · Year 1 · 6 Weeks · Feb 16 – Mar 27, 2027

NEGO 250

Negotiation & Conflict Resolution

Upcoming — Q3 Year 1
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Required at Harvard, Wharton, and Stanford for a reason: every significant outcome in your career will be the result of a negotiation. Your next compensation package. The terms of your startup's seed round. The price Okta pays to acquire a company. The partnership agreement with your biggest enterprise customer. The research on negotiation outcomes is stark — people who have been trained in BATNA analysis, anchoring, and integrative bargaining consistently out-earn and out-deal those who haven't. This 6-week course has the highest dollar-per-hour ROI of any course in this program.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply BATNA, ZOPA, and reservation price analysis to any negotiation
  • Distinguish distributive and integrative bargaining strategies and when to use each
  • Recognize and counter anchoring, framing, and cognitive bias tactics
  • Lead multi-party negotiations and design deals that create joint value
  • Apply negotiation skills to M&A, partnerships, executive compensation, and fundraising

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
Foundations — Distributive vs. Integrative Bargaining
Open →
Topics:
  • Distributive (fixed pie, zero-sum) vs. integrative (expanding the pie, value creation) bargaining
  • Negotiation as information management
  • The Dual Concern Model
  • Why most people default to distributive when integrative is almost always available
  • Introduction to the Harvard Negotiation Project
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1
  • Identify a recent negotiation (salary, vendor, job offer). Was it distributive or integrative? What value was left on the table?
Week 2
BATNA, ZOPA & Reservation Points — Mapping the Negotiation Space
Topics:
  • Mapping the negotiation space (BATNA/ZOPA/reservation price)
  • How to calculate your BATNA before any negotiation
  • Strengthening your BATNA vs weakening theirs
  • Applying BATNA to a vendor contract at Okta
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 2
  • BATNA analysis for a real or hypothetical negotiation
  • Reflection
Week 3
Anchoring, Framing & Psychological Biases in Negotiation
Topics:
  • Anchoring effect and first-offer strategy
  • Framing (gain vs loss framing)
  • Reactive devaluation
  • Overconfidence bias in negotiations
  • Debiasing techniques
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 3
  • Anchoring exercise: practice first-offer strategy in 3 scenarios
  • Reflection
Week 4
Multi-Party Negotiations, Coalition Building & Deal Design
Topics:
  • Coalition formation in multi-party deals
  • Issue packaging and log-rolling
  • Designing deal structure (contingencies, earn-outs, milestones)
  • M&A deal structure as multi-party negotiation
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 4
  • Multi-party negotiation simulation debrief
  • Deal design exercise
Week 5
Business Negotiations — M&A, Partnerships, Compensation & Fundraising
Topics:
  • M&A negotiation sequence (NDA → LOI → definitive agreement)
  • Executive compensation negotiation frameworks
  • Salary negotiation for senior engineering roles
  • Partnership agreement negotiation
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 5
  • Mid-course: M&A negotiation simulation — acquire a company at the best price
  • Debrief memo
Week 6 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Live Negotiation Simulation
Topics:
  • Live negotiation simulation — full M&A deal from initial offer to signed term sheet
  • Apply BATNA, anchoring, multi-party, and deal design frameworks in a realistic scenario
Deliverables:
  • Final: Negotiation simulation (live exercise + debrief)
  • Negotiation strategy memo (pre-negotiation plan)
  • Post-negotiation analysis
