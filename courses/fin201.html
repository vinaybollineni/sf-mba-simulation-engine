FIN 201 — Corporate Finance & Valuation

Quarter 2 · Year 1 · 10 Weeks · Nov 2, 2026 – Jan 8, 2027

FIN 201

Corporate Finance & Valuation

Upcoming — Q2 · Year 1
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

As an engineering manager, you already think in systems and trade-offs. Corporate finance is just that applied to capital allocation: where should money go to generate the highest return? By the end of this course, you will be able to build a full DCF model for any company, understand exactly why Okta's stock trades at the multiple it does, and have the financial vocabulary to speak credibly with investors and CFOs. This is the most quantitative course in Year 1 — your engineering mind will handle it easily.

Learning Objectives

  • Master time value of money: PV, FV, annuities, perpetuities
  • Build capital budgeting decisions using NPV, IRR, and payback period
  • Understand risk and return: CAPM, beta, and market risk premium
  • Calculate WACC and understand optimal capital structure
  • Build a complete DCF valuation model and comparable company analysis
  • Value a SaaS startup using multiple valuation methodologies

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Starts Nov 2
Time Value of Money — PV, FV & Annuities
Open →
Topics:
  • Present value & future value
  • Discount rates
  • Annuity formulas & perpetuities
  • Why $1 today > $1 tomorrow
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1 (due Nov 6)
  • TVM exercises using real Okta contract data
  • Apply to: evaluating a job offer with deferred equity
Week 2
Capital Budgeting — NPV, IRR & Payback
Locked
Net present value (NPV), internal rate of return (IRR), payback period, profitability index. When NPV and IRR give conflicting answers (and what to do). Apply to: should Okta build vs. buy a capability? Deliverables: Problem Set 2, Build-vs-buy NPV analysis for a real engineering decision.
Week 3
Risk & Return — CAPM, Beta & Portfolio Theory
Locked
Expected return, systematic vs. unsystematic risk, beta, Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), Security Market Line. Equity risk premium. Why Okta's high beta matters to investors and to you as a potential future equity holder. Deliverables: Problem Set 3, Look up Okta's beta and calculate expected return using CAPM.
Week 4
Cost of Capital — WACC
Locked
Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), cost of equity (CAPM), cost of debt (after-tax), optimal capital structure, Modigliani-Miller theorem. How to estimate WACC for a public company and why it matters for valuation. Deliverables: Problem Set 4, Calculate Okta's WACC using public data.
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
Capital Structure — Debt vs. Equity
Locked
Financial leverage, debt capacity, trade-off theory, pecking order theory. Why some companies are loaded with debt (LBOs) and others have none (SaaS). Capital structure decisions at Okta vs. traditional enterprise software. Deliverables: Problem Set 5 + Mid-Course: Capital structure comparison analysis (Okta vs. Salesforce vs. Microsoft).
Week 6
Dividend Policy & Share Buybacks
Locked
Dividend irrelevance theorem, signaling theory, share repurchase programs, payout ratio analysis. Why tech companies rarely pay dividends. Okta's buyback program and what it signals about management's confidence. Deliverables: Problem Set 6, Analyze Okta's capital return history.
Week 7
DCF Valuation — Building a Full Model
Locked
Free cash flow to firm (FCFF), terminal value (Gordon Growth + exit multiple), sensitivity analysis, football field chart. Build a complete 5-year DCF model from scratch for a real company. Deliverables: Problem Set 7, Build DCF model for Okta using the FY2025 10-K.
Week 8
Comparable Company Analysis (Comps)
Locked
EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, P/E, EV/FCF multiples, selecting comparable companies, precedent transaction analysis, the "football field" valuation summary. Why SaaS companies trade on revenue multiples, not earnings. Deliverables: Problem Set 8, Comps table for Okta vs. CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Palo Alto.
Week 9
Real Options & Strategic Investment Decisions
Locked
Limitations of NPV, option to expand/delay/abandon, Black-Scholes intuition (no math required), real options in tech: R&D investment, platform bets, platform adjacency decisions. When to use real options thinking vs. DCF. Deliverables: Problem Set 9, Real options analysis: Okta's decision to expand into Workforce Identity vs. Customer Identity.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Value a SaaS Company from Scratch
Locked
Final Deliverable: Full valuation report on a public SaaS company of your choice (not Okta). Must include: (1) DCF model with assumptions clearly stated, (2) Comps table with 4 comparable companies, (3) Football field valuation summary, (4) Implied price vs. current market price, (5) 2-page investment memo: Buy / Hold / Sell with thesis. Due Jan 8, 2027.
