ENT 501 — New Venture Creation & Business Planning

Quarter 5 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Aug 4 – Oct 7, 2027

ENT 501

New Venture Creation & Business Planning

Upcoming — Quarter 5
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Welcome to Year 2. You've completed the core MBA toolkit — finance, strategy, marketing, ops, entrepreneurship foundations. Now we build something real. ENT 501 is where the rubber meets the road: legal structure, cap tables, SAFE notes, founding team dynamics, and your first real fundraising strategy. You have a technical moat that 99% of startup founders don't have. This course is about converting that advantage into investor capital and company equity.

Learning Objectives

  • Structure a company legally: Delaware C-Corp, equity splits, vesting schedules, 83(b) elections
  • Build and manage a cap table through multiple funding rounds
  • Understand SAFE notes, convertible notes, and priced equity rounds
  • Design a founding team framework — technical co-founder, roles, equity negotiation
  • Build a fundraising strategy: narrative, deck, data room, and investor targeting list

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Active Now
From Idea to Company — Incorporation, Equity & Founder Vesting
Open →
Topics:
  • Delaware C-Corp incorporation
  • Equity splits between co-founders
  • 4-year vesting / 1-year cliff
  • 83(b) election importance
Deliverables:
  • Draft founding team equity structure (due Fri Aug 8)
  • Reflection: who is your co-founder and why (due Sun Aug 10)
Week 2
Cap Table Basics — Shares, Dilution & Option Pools
Locked
Fully diluted shares, option pools, dilution mechanics, how a Series A changes ownership percentages. Build a cap table in Excel: seed through Series A. Understand how the option pool shuffle affects founder ownership before a priced round.
Week 3
SAFE Notes, Convertible Notes & Priced Rounds
Locked
YC SAFE mechanics; valuation caps and discounts; how YC-style SAFEs work in practice; when to use a convertible note vs. a priced round; pro-rata rights and why they matter to early investors. Model the conversion math on a SAFE with a $10M cap.
Week 4
Building a Founding Team — Equity Negotiation & Roles
Locked
Technical vs. business co-founders; the "Why this team?" investor question; equity negotiation playbook; vesting cliff disputes and how to handle them gracefully. Case: How Stripe structured its founding team equity in 2010.
Week 5 · Mid-Project Due
Product-Market Fit Frameworks
Locked
Sean Ellis test; retention curves as PMF signal; how to know when you have it vs. when you're fooling yourself. Mid-project due: Evidence of PMF for your startup concept — customer interviews, retention data, or validated demand signals.
Week 6
Early Customer Acquisition & Founder-Led Sales
Locked
Founder-led sales as a technical founder; how to close your first 10 enterprise customers; Okta's early enterprise sales playbook as a case study. Building a sales motion before you can afford a sales team. The "do things that don't scale" philosophy.
Week 7
Seed Fundraising Strategy — Building Your Investor List
Locked
Target list building; warm intro vs. cold outreach; first VC meeting prep; the "signal vs. noise" problem in fundraising; how to run a tight process with multiple term sheets. What seed investors are actually pattern-matching on.
Week 8
Investor Relations & Due Diligence
Locked
What goes in a data room; financial due diligence for a pre-revenue startup; technical due diligence red flags (architecture, security, IP ownership); reference checks from both directions. How to keep a process moving without losing momentum.
Week 9
Board Governance for Startups
Locked
First board composition; board meeting cadence and format; investor information rights; protective provisions and consent rights; board observer seats. How to run a board meeting as a first-time CEO — and what founders give up at Series A.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Fundraising-Ready Business Plan — Full Investor Package
Locked
Final Project: Complete investor package for your startup. Deliver: 15-slide pitch deck, 3-year financial model, 1-page executive summary, data room index, and a target investor list with a customized pitch thesis for each firm. Due: Oct 7, 2027.
