ENT 501 — New Venture Creation & Business Planning

Quarter 5 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Aug 4 – Oct 7, 2027

ENT 501

New Venture Creation & Business Planning

Upcoming — Quarter 5
Professor's Welcome Note:

Welcome to Year 2. You've completed the core MBA toolkit — finance, strategy, marketing, ops, entrepreneurship foundations. Now we build something real. ENT 501 is where the rubber meets the road: legal structure, cap tables, SAFE notes, founding team dynamics, and your first real fundraising strategy. You have a technical moat that 99% of startup founders don't have. This course is about converting that advantage into investor capital and company equity.

Learning Objectives

  • Structure a company legally: Delaware C-Corp, equity splits, vesting schedules, 83(b) elections
  • Build and manage a cap table through multiple funding rounds
  • Understand SAFE notes, convertible notes, and priced equity rounds
  • Design a founding team framework — technical co-founder, roles, equity negotiation
  • Build a fundraising strategy: narrative, deck, data room, and investor targeting list
