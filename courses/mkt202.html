MKT 202 — Marketing Management

Quarter 2 · Year 1 · 10 Weeks · Feb 22 – May 1, 2027

MKT 202

Marketing Management

Upcoming — Q2 Year 1
Professor's Welcome Note:

Engineering managers often dismiss marketing as "soft." That's a costly mistake for anyone who wants to found a company or lead a business unit. Marketing is the system that connects your product to the market — and just like engineering systems, it has inputs, outputs, and measurable conversion rates. By the end of this course, you'll be able to write a complete go-to-market strategy that could be presented to a board, understand exactly why Okta's B2B marketing motion works, and design the positioning strategy for your future startup. This is where technical people gain an unfair advantage: you understand the product deeply, and now you'll learn how to sell it.

Learning Objectives

  • Develop market segmentation, targeting, and positioning (STP) strategies for tech products
  • Build a complete go-to-market plan including channel strategy, messaging, and success metrics
  • Understand B2B enterprise sales cycles and how marketing and sales interlock
  • Apply digital marketing analytics: CAC, LTV, payback period, NRR
  • Design a product launch strategy from zero to first 100 customers

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
Marketing Fundamentals — The 4Ps & Customer Value
Topics:
  • Product, Price, Place, Promotion
  • Value proposition design
  • Customer value vs. product features
  • Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework
  • Why "build it and they will come" fails
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1
  • Value proposition for Okta's Workforce Identity product using JTBD framework
Week 2
Market Research & Consumer Behavior
Topics:
  • Primary vs secondary research methods
  • Qualitative interviews and focus groups
  • Survey design and sampling
  • Customer persona development for identity security buyers
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 2
  • Customer interview guide for an IAM product
  • Reflection
Week 3
STP — Segmentation, Targeting & Positioning
Topics:
  • Market segmentation variables (demographic/psychographic/behavioral/firmographic)
  • Targeting frameworks (segment attractiveness)
  • Positioning map construction
  • STP analysis for Okta's Workforce vs Customer Identity products
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 3
  • Full STP analysis for an identity security product
  • Reflection
Week 4
Brand Strategy & Brand Equity
Topics:
  • Brand equity components (Keller's CBBE model)
  • Brand architecture (house of brands vs branded house)
  • B2B brand trust and its impact on enterprise sales
  • Okta's brand strategy vs Microsoft Entra
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 4
  • Brand equity audit for Okta
  • Reflection
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
B2B Marketing — Enterprise Sales Cycles
Topics:
  • B2B sales cycle stages (awareness → evaluation → purchase → renewal)
  • Enterprise vs SMB go-to-market motion
  • Sales-assisted vs product-led in B2B
  • Champion/economic buyer/technical buyer mapping
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 5
  • Mid-course: Enterprise GTM analysis for a B2B SaaS product
  • Stakeholder map for an enterprise deal
Week 6
Digital Marketing & PLG — SEO, SEM, Product-Led Growth, Content
Topics:
  • SEO fundamentals for B2B SaaS
  • Product-led growth (PLG) mechanics (freemium/free trial/reverse trial)
  • Content marketing funnel (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)
  • How Auth0's developer PLG motion worked
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 6
  • PLG audit for a product you use daily
  • SEO keyword analysis exercise
Week 7
Pricing Strategy
Topics:
  • Pricing strategy frameworks (cost-plus/value-based/competitive)
  • Price elasticity in enterprise SaaS
  • Psychological pricing and anchoring
  • Okta's tiered pricing model analysis
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 7
  • Pricing analysis: Okta vs Ping vs CyberArk
  • Reflection
Week 8
Product Launch & GTM Execution
Topics:
  • Product launch framework (pre/launch/post)
  • Beta programs and early adopter strategy
  • Launch metrics (MQLs, pipeline, ARR)
  • Coordinating eng/product/marketing/sales for a launch
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 8
  • Product launch plan for a hypothetical IAM feature
  • Reflection
Week 9
Customer Acquisition & Retention Metrics — CAC, LTV, NRR
Topics:
  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) calculation
  • Lifetime Value (LTV) modeling
  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
  • CAC Payback Period
  • LTV:CAC benchmarks for enterprise SaaS
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 9
  • Unit economics model for a SaaS startup (Excel)
  • Reflection
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Full Go-to-Market Plan for a SaaS Product
Topics:
  • Full go-to-market strategy for your startup idea
  • STP analysis, positioning statement, pricing model
  • Channel strategy, launch plan
  • Unit economics
Deliverables:
  • Final: Complete GTM strategy document (due date)
  • GTM one-pager
  • Financial assumptions summary
