CAP 802 — MBA Capstone: Launch Your Venture

Quarter 8 · Year 2 · 12 Weeks · May 5 – Jul 25, 2028

CAP 802

MBA Capstone: Launch Your Venture

Upcoming
Professor's Welcome Note:

This is it. Two years. 22 courses. $700 and no debt. You built something that no MBA program can give you: a portfolio of real work, a complete business plan, and the credibility of someone who actually did the hard thing. The capstone is your final statement: a fully investor-ready startup package built on everything you've learned. Present it like you're walking into a real Series A meeting — because someday, you will be.

Learning Objectives

  • Synthesize all 2 years of MBA learning into one coherent venture
  • Build a complete, investor-ready startup package: plan, financial model, and pitch deck
  • Demonstrate MBA-level thinking across finance, strategy, marketing, operations, and leadership
  • Present your startup as if pitching to a real investor panel
  • Complete your simulated MBA with a portfolio artifact you can show to employers, investors, and co-founders

Weekly Syllabus

Weeks 1-2 · Active Now
Problem & Solution
Topics:
  • Finalize your startup thesis
  • Problem definition and scope
  • Customer identification
  • Unique positioning analysis
Deliverables:
  • Refined problem statement + 10 customer validation quotes (due Fri May 9)
  • Competitive landscape map (due Sun May 11)
Weeks 3-4
Market Research & Competitive Analysis
Topics:
  • TAM/SAM/SOM with primary + secondary research
  • Competitive matrix (5 competitors × 6 dimensions)
  • Porter's Five Forces for your specific market
  • "Why we win" one-paragraph competitive positioning
Deliverables:
  • Market research report (due)
  • Competitive matrix spreadsheet
  • Competitive positioning statement
Weeks 5-6
Business Model, Pricing & Unit Economics
Topics:
  • Revenue model decision and pricing strategy
  • Unit economics (CAC/LTV/payback)
  • How your business model creates defensibility
  • Sensitivity analysis on key assumptions
Deliverables:
  • Business model and pricing document (due)
  • Unit economics model (Excel)
  • Revenue model assumptions
Weeks 7-8
Financial Model — 3-Year Projections
Topics:
  • Monthly P&L Year 1 (month-by-month)
  • Annual projections Year 2-3
  • Funding requirements and use of proceeds
  • Break-even analysis, scenario modeling (base/bull/bear)
Deliverables:
  • 3-year financial model (due)
  • Monthly cash flow spreadsheet
  • Scenario analysis
Weeks 9-10
Go-to-Market & Growth Strategy
Topics:
  • First 100 customers acquisition plan
  • Channel strategy and budget
  • Partnership and distribution approach
  • PLG vs sales-led decision, international expansion timeline
Deliverables:
  • GTM and growth strategy document (due)
  • Channel model
  • 12-month pipeline projection
Week 11
Pitch Deck
Topics:
  • 20-slide investor deck using Sequoia/YC structure: problem → solution → market → product → traction → model → competition → team → financials → ask
Deliverables:
  • Pitch deck (20 slides, due)
  • Deck narrative document
  • Practice run notes
Week 12 · CAPSTONE DUE — July 25, 2028
Final Presentation — Simulated Investor Panel
Topics:
  • Simulated investor panel presentation — 10 minutes pitch + Q&A. This is your MBA graduation moment. Complete package: 30-40 page plan + 20-slide deck + 3-year model + 10-minute recorded pitch
Deliverables:
  • FINAL: Complete MBA capstone (due July 25, 2028)
  • Business plan document (30-40 pages)
  • Pitch recording
  • Self-assessment reflection
