LEAD 701 — Executive Leadership & Organizational Change

Quarter 7 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Feb 4 – Apr 9, 2028

LEAD 701

Executive Leadership & Organizational Change

Upcoming
Open Course →
Professor's Welcome Note:

You're almost there. Two years in, one quarter to go before your capstone — and this is the course that prepares you for the hardest part of the exec journey: the transition from manager to leader. Andy Grove built Intel by treating management as engineering. The First 90 Days is the playbook every incoming CTO, CEO, and VP uses. By Week 10, you'll have your personal 100-day executive transition plan — ready to use the next time you step into a new leadership role.

Learning Objectives

  • Navigate the CTO/CEO transition: what changes at the VP+ level and how to adapt fast
  • Build and manage executive teams: hiring principles, trust-building, and letting people go with dignity
  • Manage board and investor relationships: communication cadence, what they actually need, how to handle bad news
  • Lead large-scale organizational change using Kotter's 8-step model and behavioral change science
  • Develop your executive communication style: boardroom presence, difficult conversations, and crisis messaging
