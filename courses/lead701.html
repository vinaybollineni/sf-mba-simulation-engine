LEAD 701 — Executive Leadership & Organizational Change

Quarter 7 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Feb 4 – Apr 9, 2028

LEAD 701

Executive Leadership & Organizational Change

Upcoming
Go to Week 1 Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

You're almost there. Two years in, one quarter to go before your capstone — and this is the course that prepares you for the hardest part of the exec journey: the transition from manager to leader. Andy Grove built Intel by treating management as engineering. The First 90 Days is the playbook every incoming CTO, CEO, and VP uses. By Week 10, you'll have your personal 100-day executive transition plan — ready to use the next time you step into a new leadership role.

Learning Objectives

  • Navigate the CTO/CEO transition: what changes at the VP+ level and how to adapt fast
  • Build and manage executive teams: hiring principles, trust-building, and letting people go with dignity
  • Manage board and investor relationships: communication cadence, what they actually need, how to handle bad news
  • Lead large-scale organizational change using Kotter's 8-step model and behavioral change science
  • Develop your executive communication style: boardroom presence, difficult conversations, and crisis messaging

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Active Now
The CEO/CTO Transition — What Changes at the Top
Open →
Topics:
  • From doing to directing
  • From certainty to ambiguity
  • From team to organization
  • The three executive shifts
Deliverables:

  • The First 90 Days Ch. 1-3 reflection (due Sun Feb 10)
Week 2
Building Executive Teams — Hiring, Firing, Trust
Locked
How great execs hire differently; the "brilliant jerk" problem; how to let people go gracefully; building trust as a new exec who inherited someone else's team.
Week 3
Board Management & Investor Communication
Locked
What boards actually need vs. what CEOs think they need; monthly investor updates that work; how to deliver bad news before the board meeting; the difference between a management board and a governance board.
Week 4
Organizational Design — Structure Follows Strategy
Locked
Functional vs. divisional vs. matrix structures; how Okta is organized and why; Conway's Law for tech orgs; when to reorg and when not to.
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
Leading Large-Scale Change — Kotter's 8 Steps
Locked
Creating urgency; building a coalition; why 70% of change initiatives fail; the role of quick wins; how Satya Nadella's growth mindset shift at Microsoft is the canonical case. Mid-project: Map a real organizational change initiative and apply Kotter.
Week 6
Executive Communication — Storytelling, Presence, Influence
Locked
The McKinsey presentation structure; Barbara Minto's pyramid principle; how to run a board meeting; the 5-minute weekly exec update format.
Week 7
Culture Building — Values, Rituals, and Accountability
Locked
How culture is built (not declared); the "culture fit" vs. "culture add" distinction; building a high-accountability culture without micromanagement; how Ben Horowitz's "The Hard Thing About Hard Things" applies to Okta's culture.
Week 8 · Case: Okta Breach 2022
Managing in Crisis
Locked
The Okta October 2022 security breach as a case study in crisis management; incident communication to customers, employees, and investors; reputation repair; what great crisis leadership looks like vs. what bad crisis leadership looks like.
Week 9
Personal Board of Directors & Executive Coaching
Locked
Who should be on your personal advisory board; how to find and use executive coaches; feedback systems for leaders who rarely get honest feedback; building a 5-year network plan.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
100-Day Executive Transition Plan
Locked
Final Project: Your personal plan for stepping into a VP, CTO, or CEO role: first 30/60/90 days; stakeholder mapping; quick wins; culture assessment; communication plan; decision rights. Due: April 9, 2028.
