GLOB 250 — Global Business & Macroeconomic Environment

Quarter 2 · Year 1 · 6 Weeks · Jan 11 – Feb 19, 2027

GLOB 250

Global Business & Macroeconomic Environment

Upcoming — Q2 · Year 1
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Okta operates in 19 countries and earns 25%+ of revenue internationally. Every executive decision you'll make — from pricing to hiring to partnership strategy — will be influenced by macroeconomic forces you need to understand: currency moves, interest rate cycles, trade policy, and geopolitical risk. Harvard makes this course mandatory (they call it BGIE — Business, Government & the International Economy) because executives who don't understand macro make decisions in a vacuum. This 6-week course is the fastest way to close that gap.

Learning Objectives

  • Interpret GDP, inflation, interest rate, and employment data and assess their impact on business strategy
  • Understand international trade theory and how tariff/trade policy affects company supply chains and pricing
  • Analyze foreign exchange risk and identify hedging strategies for international revenue exposure
  • Apply the CAGE Distance Framework to evaluate international market entry decisions
  • Assess geopolitical and country-level risk for emerging market expansion

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Starts Jan 11
Macroeconomics for Executives — GDP, Inflation & Business Cycles
Open →
Topics:
  • GDP components (C+I+G+NX)
  • Business cycles & indicators
  • CPI, PCE inflation measures
  • Fed funds rate & transmission to business
Deliverables:
  • Macro Dashboard (1-page memo)
  • Summarize US conditions using 5 key indicators (BLS, Fed, BEA)
Week 2
International Trade Theory, Globalization & Supply Chains
Locked
Comparative advantage, trade balances, WTO framework, recent deglobalization trends, reshoring. How tariffs affect SaaS companies (mostly indirectly through customer input costs). Supply chain resilience post-COVID as a strategic issue. Deliverables: Problem Set 2, Identify how current US-China trade tensions could affect Okta's go-to-market in Asia.
Week 3
Foreign Exchange, Currency Risk & Hedging Strategy
Locked
Exchange rate systems (fixed vs. floating), PPP theory, FX risk types (transaction, translation, economic). Natural hedging vs. financial hedging (forward contracts, options). How Okta reports international revenue in USD and what currency tailwinds/headwinds mean for earnings. Deliverables: Problem Set 3, Analyze Okta's FX exposure using the FY2025 10-K Notes to Financial Statements.
Week 4
Geopolitical Risk & the CAGE Distance Framework
Locked
Country risk assessment (political, economic, legal, operational). Ghemawat's CAGE Distance Framework: Cultural, Administrative, Geographic, Economic dimensions. Scoring markets using CAGE. Why Salesforce entered Germany before Brazil despite Brazil being larger. Deliverables: CAGE Analysis — score 3 potential expansion markets for Okta (UK, India, Japan) on all 4 dimensions.
Week 5
Emerging Markets Strategy — India, Southeast Asia, Middle East
Locked
Emerging market characteristics, informal economy, leapfrog technology adoption, regulatory complexity, local competition dynamics. India's identity/security market opportunity (massive, underpenetrated). Southeast Asia's digital economy. Middle East cloud adoption. Deliverables: Market Brief — 2-page assessment of ONE emerging market opportunity for Okta. Include: market size, CAGE score, key risks, recommended entry mode.
Week 6 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Global Market Entry Analysis
Locked
Final Deliverable: Global Expansion Strategy Memo (4 pages). Choose a real company (SaaS preferred) that has NOT yet expanded to a specific market. Recommend whether and how they should enter. Cover: CAGE Distance analysis, macro environment, currency risk, regulatory risk, entry mode recommendation (greenfield/acquisition/partnership), and first-year milestones. Due Feb 19, 2027.
