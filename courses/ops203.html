OPS 203 — Operations & Process Management

Quarter 2 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · May 5 – Jun 26, 2027

OPS 203

Operations & Process Management

Upcoming — Q2 Year 1
Professor's Welcome Note:

You run a data platform team — which means you already live in the world of SLAs, on-call rotations, incident response, and capacity planning. Operations management takes those instincts and gives them a rigorous framework that scales beyond engineering. The Toyota Production System, Lean thinking, and the Theory of Constraints apply just as directly to a software delivery pipeline as to a car assembly line. After this course, you'll see every process you touch — from sprint planning to QBRs to vendor negotiations — through the lens of efficiency, capacity, and throughput. That's the operations mindset every executive needs.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply process flow analysis to identify bottlenecks and throughput constraints
  • Use capacity planning frameworks to optimize resource allocation
  • Apply Lean and Six Sigma thinking to eliminate waste in software and business operations
  • Understand supply chain fundamentals and their SaaS equivalents
  • Build an operational improvement plan for a real business process

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
Process Flow Analysis & Bottlenecks
Topics:
  • Process flow diagrams
  • Throughput rate & cycle time
  • Little's Law (WIP = throughput × cycle time)
  • Bottleneck analysis, capacity utilization
  • Apply to: software delivery, onboarding, incidents
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1
  • Map your team's software delivery process as a flow diagram; identify the bottleneck
Week 2
Capacity Planning & Resource Management
Topics:
  • Capacity utilization and bottleneck identification
  • Resource pooling and queue theory basics
  • Headcount planning for engineering teams
  • Cloud infrastructure capacity as an ops decision
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 2
  • Capacity analysis for Okta's on-call engineering team
  • Reflection
Week 3
Lean Thinking & Waste Elimination
Topics:
  • Toyota Production System principles
  • Lean thinking: value stream mapping
  • 8 wastes (MUDA)
  • Applying Lean to software delivery pipelines
  • Kaizen and continuous improvement culture
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 3
  • Value stream map for a software feature delivery process
  • Reflection
Week 4 · Mid-Course Project Due
Quality Management — Six Sigma & TQM
Topics:
  • Six Sigma DMAIC framework
  • Statistical process control
  • Total Quality Management (TQM)
  • Quality metrics for SaaS: error budgets, SLAs, p99 latency
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 4
  • Mid-course: Quality analysis for an Okta engineering process
  • Error budget calculation exercise
Week 5
Supply Chain Fundamentals
Topics:
  • Supply chain fundamentals (demand/supply/inventory)
  • Just-in-time vs just-in-case inventory
  • For SaaS: vendor dependency and API supply chains
  • Third-party risk management
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 5
  • Supply chain risk assessment for a SaaS product's API dependencies
  • Reflection
Week 6
Agile Operations & OKRs — From Delivery to Business Rhythm
Topics:
  • Critical path method (CPM) for project planning
  • Agile vs Waterfall trade-offs
  • OKR framework for engineering teams
  • How Okta runs quarterly planning
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 6
  • Critical path analysis for a software release
  • OKR set for a hypothetical engineering quarter
Week 7
SaaS Operational Metrics — SLAs, Uptime & Incident Management
Topics:
  • SLA definition and measurement
  • Incident management frameworks (SEV1-4)
  • Uptime/availability metrics (99.99% = 52 minutes downtime/year)
  • SaaS operational dashboards
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 7
  • SLA framework design for a new SaaS product
  • Incident postmortem template
Week 8 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Operational Transformation Plan
Topics:
  • Complete operational improvement plan for a real business process
  • Map current state, identify waste
  • Design future state
  • Quantify financial impact
Deliverables:
  • Final: Operations transformation plan (due date)
  • Process map (current vs future state)
  • ROI calculation
