TEC 401 — Technology Strategy & Digital Transformation

Quarter 4 · Year 1 · 10 Weeks · Apr 5 – Jun 11, 2027

TEC 401

Technology Strategy & Digital Transformation

Upcoming — Quarter 4 · Year 1
View Curriculum →
Professor's Welcome Note:

You're already on the technical side of this course. The goal here is different: we're training you to be the person in the boardroom who can translate technology decisions into business value — and business strategy into technical direction. As Okta's engineering manager, you understand cloud architecture. By the end of TEC 401, you'll know how to communicate that in terms of competitive moats, revenue impact, and investor narratives. This is the course that bridges your engineering identity to your executive future.

Learning Objectives

  • Build and communicate a technology strategy roadmap tied to business outcomes
  • Evaluate AI/ML as a strategic asset and design an AI adoption strategy
  • Lead digital transformation programs — culture, process, and tech together
  • Evaluate build vs. buy vs. partner decisions using frameworks and financial analysis
  • Position technology investments to boards, investors, and non-technical stakeholders

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Active Now
Technology as Competitive Moat
Open →
Topics:
  • Network effects and switching costs
  • Data moats and API lock-in
  • Okta's identity graph as a strategic asset
  • Moat frameworks: Porter vs. Hamilton Helmer
Deliverables:
  • Technology moat analysis for Okta (due Fri Apr 9)
  • Reflection on your biggest build-vs-buy decision (due Sun Apr 11)
Week 2
Platform Business Models
Locked
AWS, Salesforce, and Okta as platforms — why platform beats pipeline every time. API-first architecture as a business strategy. How Okta's integration catalog creates ecosystem lock-in and why that matters to investors.
Week 3
API Economy & Ecosystem Strategy
Locked
Developer-led growth, API monetization, and how Stripe, Twilio, and Plaid built billion-dollar businesses through developer ecosystems. How to build a flywheel where developers become your sales force. Apply the playbook to identity.
Week 4
AI/ML Strategy — When to Build, Buy, or Partner
Locked
Foundation models vs. fine-tuning vs. RAG — how to choose. AI product decisions in a world with OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-source alternatives. Ethical and compliance considerations specific to identity AI: bias in authentication, privacy, and regulatory exposure.
Week 5 · Mid-Project Due
Data as a Strategic Asset
Locked
Data governance, data moats, and Okta's identity data advantage — what it enables that competitors can't replicate. Privacy-preserving analytics and synthetic data for ML training. Mid-course project due: build/buy/partner analysis for a real technology decision.
Week 6
Cloud Architecture Decisions & Business Impact
Locked
Multi-cloud strategy, total cost of ownership, and FinOps for both enterprises and startups. How architecture decisions show up in gross margins and investor models. Translate your infrastructure choices into CFO language.
Week 7
Digital Transformation — Culture, Process, Technology
Locked
Kotter's 8-step change model applied to real technology transformations. Why 70% of digital transformation projects fail — and what the successful 30% do differently. Leading change without authority as an engineering manager.
Week 8
Cybersecurity & Identity as Business Enablers
Locked
Zero trust architecture reframed as a growth story, not a cost center. How Okta sells security as productivity. Regulatory tailwinds — SOX, GDPR, NIST — that create structural demand for identity solutions. Case: Okta's enterprise sales motion.
Week 9
Tech M&A — Evaluating Acquisitions
Locked
How to evaluate a technology acquisition: technology fit, team retention, integration risk, and strategic upside. Okta's $6.5B acquisition of Auth0 as a deep case study — what they bought, what it cost, and whether it was worth it. Acqui-hire vs. strategic buy frameworks.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Full Technology Strategy for Your Startup
Locked
Final Tech Strategy: Choose your startup idea and build the complete technology strategy — architecture choices, build/buy/partner decisions, AI roadmap, competitive moat analysis, and a 3-year technology roadmap. Due: June 11, 2027.
