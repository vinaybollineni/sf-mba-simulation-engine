ENT 402 — Entrepreneurship Foundations

Quarter 4 · Year 1 · 10 Weeks · Apr 5 – Jun 11, 2027

ENT 402

Entrepreneurship Foundations

Upcoming — Quarter 4 · Year 1
View Curriculum →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Every year, a handful of Okta engineers leave to start companies. Most of them fail within 18 months — not because the technology is bad, but because they've never had to sell, raise money, or find product-market fit. This course is the antidote. We'll apply the same rigor you bring to system design to the problem of building a company. By Week 10, you'll have your first investor pitch deck — and you'll know exactly what questions a Series A investor will ask before you walk in the door.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply lean startup methodology to validate ideas before building
  • Develop and stress-test a Business Model Canvas for a real startup idea
  • Conduct 10+ customer discovery interviews using the Jobs-to-be-Done framework
  • Build a credible investor pitch deck and present it confidently
  • Understand the economics of startup fundraising: pre-seed, seed, and Series A
