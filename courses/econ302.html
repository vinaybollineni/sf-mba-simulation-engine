ECON 302 — Managerial Economics

Quarter 3 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · Dec 14, 2026 – Feb 5, 2027

ECON 302

Managerial Economics

Upcoming — Starts Dec 14, 2026
Professor's Welcome Note:

Economics is the engine under every good business decision. As someone who ships data pipelines at Okta, you already think in systems — supply, demand, and equilibrium are just another system. This course will give you the pricing intuition, incentive design skills, and market analysis frameworks that distinguish good executives from great ones. By Week 8, you'll be able to redesign any SaaS pricing model from first principles — and back it up with data.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply microeconomic principles — supply, demand, elasticity — to pricing and market strategy
  • Understand market structures (perfect competition → monopoly) and their implications for competitive behavior
  • Design pricing strategies: bundling, versioning, freemium, and two-part tariffs for SaaS products
  • Apply game theory to predict competitor responses and design strategic moves
  • Build incentive systems that align employee behavior with organizational goals

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
Supply, Demand & Market Equilibrium
Topics:
  • Market clearing and equilibrium price
  • Shifts vs. movements along curves
  • How macro cycles hit SaaS demand
  • Demand signals in identity security
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1 (due Fri Dec 18)
  • Okta demand analysis mini-case (due Sun Dec 20)
  • Reflection prompt
Week 2
Elasticity & Pricing Decisions
Topics:
  • Price elasticity of demand formula
  • Income elasticity and cross-price elasticity
  • Elasticity for enterprise SaaS (inelastic due to switching costs)
  • How Okta can raise prices without losing enterprise customers
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 2
  • Elasticity analysis for Okta's pricing
  • Reflection
Week 3
Production & Cost Functions
Topics:
  • Short-run vs long-run production functions
  • Marginal cost and average total cost curves
  • Economies of scale in cloud infrastructure
  • Why SaaS has near-zero marginal cost for additional users
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 3
  • Cost function analysis for a SaaS company
  • Reflection
Week 4
Market Structures — From Competition to Monopoly
Topics:
  • Perfect competition vs monopolistic competition vs oligopoly vs monopoly
  • How identity security market structure is evolving
  • Market concentration and HHI index
  • Microsoft's bundling as anti-competitive behavior
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 4
  • Market structure analysis for the IAM industry
  • Reflection
Week 5 · Mid-Course Case Due
Game Theory & Strategic Interaction
Topics:
  • Nash equilibrium concept
  • Prisoner's dilemma in business
  • Dominant strategies and backward induction
  • Competitive dynamics between Okta and Microsoft Entra ID
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 5
  • Mid-course: Game theory analysis of Okta's competitive strategy
  • Strategic response matrix exercise
Week 6
Pricing Strategies — Bundling, Versioning & Freemium
Topics:
  • Bundling strategy (pure/mixed bundling)
  • Versioning and price discrimination (1st/2nd/3rd degree)
  • Freemium conversion economics
  • Two-part tariffs (subscription + usage fees)
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 6
  • Pricing strategy redesign exercise for a SaaS product
  • Reflection
Week 7
Incentive Design & Principal-Agent Problems
Topics:
  • Principal-agent problem (shareholder vs manager, employer vs employee)
  • Moral hazard and adverse selection
  • Optimal compensation design (fixed vs variable pay)
  • Incentive alignment for remote engineering teams
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 7
  • Sales compensation plan design exercise
  • Engineering incentive structure analysis
Week 8 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Full Pricing Strategy Redesign
Topics:
  • Complete pricing strategy redesign for Okta or your startup — using elasticity, market structure analysis, game theory, and pricing strategy frameworks
Deliverables:
  • Final: Pricing strategy memo (due date)
  • Pricing model Excel
  • Executive recommendation (1 page)
