OB 103 — Organizational Behavior & Leadership Foundations

Quarter 1 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · Aug 4 – Sep 25, 2026

OB 103

Organizational Behavior & Leadership Foundations

Upcoming — Starts Aug 4, 2026
Professor's Welcome Note:

You've been managing an engineering team long enough to know that the hardest problems aren't technical — they're human. Why do smart people underperform? Why do some teams consistently outship others with half the headcount? Organizational behavior gives you the science behind what you've already observed in the field. By the end of this course, you'll have frameworks for every hard leadership conversation you've been avoiding, and you'll understand why the way Satya Nadella transformed Microsoft's culture is the highest-leverage thing any leader can do.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the psychological and social forces that drive individual and group behavior in organizations
  • Apply psychological safety frameworks to build high-performing engineering and cross-functional teams
  • Analyze leadership styles and select the right approach for different team maturity levels
  • Navigate organizational politics and build influence without formal authority
  • Develop a concrete, actionable personal leadership philosophy
