STR 601 — Corporate Strategy, M&A & Strategic Alliances

Quarter 6 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Nov 3, 2027 – Jan 12, 2028

STR 601

Corporate Strategy, M&A & Strategic Alliances

Upcoming — Quarter 6
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

STR 601 is where business strategy meets financial engineering. Okta's $6.5 billion acquisition of Auth0 is one of the defining M&A deals in identity security — and by the end of this course, you'll be able to dissect it completely: the strategic rationale, the valuation methodology, the deal structure, the integration plan, and what Okta paid too much for. Whether you're planning to sell your startup or buy one, this course gives you the frameworks and financial tools to navigate M&A from both sides of the table.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish corporate strategy from business unit strategy; build a portfolio strategy using BCG and GE-McKinsey matrices
  • Evaluate M&A deals from strategic, financial, and operational perspectives
  • Model M&A valuation: DCF, trading comps, precedent transactions, and accretion/dilution analysis
  • Structure and negotiate M&A deals, including LOIs, term sheets, and closing conditions
  • Design post-merger integration frameworks that actually preserve deal value

Deliverables:

Week 2
Portfolio Strategy — BCG Matrix & GE-McKinsey
Locked
Cash cows, stars, question marks, dogs; applying the BCG matrix to Okta's Workforce Identity vs. Customer Identity products; when to divest vs. invest vs. harvest. The limits of portfolio frameworks and why execution still matters.
Week 3
M&A Strategy — Why Deals Succeed and Fail
Locked
Strategic rationale for M&A (scale, scope, capabilities, markets); M&A failure statistics; the "winner's curse" in competitive auctions; why Okta overpaid for Auth0 in hindsight and what it bought in return. When inorganic growth beats organic.
Week 4
Valuation in M&A — DCF, Comps & Precedent Transactions
Locked
Football field analysis; LBO valuation basics; how deal synergies get modeled — and inflated; Rosenbaum & Pearl methodology applied to a real tech deal. Build a full valuation model for a hypothetical SaaS acquisition.
Week 5 · Mid-Project Due
Deal Structuring & Negotiation
Locked
Cash vs. stock deals; earn-outs and when they destroy trust; representations and warranties; MAC (Material Adverse Change) clauses; Okta's Auth0 deal structure in detail. Mid-project due: Full valuation analysis of a real tech M&A deal.
Week 6
Due Diligence Framework — Technical, Financial & Legal
Locked
Technical, financial, legal, commercial, and HR due diligence; red flags that kill deals; how data rooms work; what lawyers look for in representations and warranties. The engineering manager's unique advantage in technical DD.
Week 7
Post-Merger Integration — The 100-Day Plan
Locked
The 100-day integration plan; cultural integration challenges; technology stack rationalization; how Okta integrated Auth0's developer platform and identity engine. Why most M&A value is destroyed in the 12 months after close.
Week 8
Strategic Alliances & Joint Ventures
Locked
Alliance vs. acquisition decision framework; partnership negotiation; how Okta's partner ecosystem (AWS, Salesforce, Workday) works as a strategic alliance network. When a JV is smarter than full acquisition — and how to structure one.
Week 9
International Strategy — Market Entry Modes
Locked
Greenfield vs. acquisition vs. JV for international expansion; how identity companies expand to regulated markets (EMEA, APAC); GDPR and data residency as strategic constraints; the tradeoffs between speed and control in market entry.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
M&A Recommendation — Full Strategic Acquisition Package
Locked
Final Project: Choose a real company and identify a strategic acquisition target. Deliver the full recommendation: strategic rationale, valuation model (DCF + comps), deal structure, 100-day integration plan, and risk analysis. Due: Jan 12, 2028.
