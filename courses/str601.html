STR 601 — Corporate Strategy, M&A & Strategic Alliances

Quarter 6 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Nov 3, 2027 – Jan 12, 2028

STR 601

Corporate Strategy, M&A & Strategic Alliances

Upcoming — Quarter 6
Professor's Welcome Note:

STR 601 is where business strategy meets financial engineering. Okta's $6.5 billion acquisition of Auth0 is one of the defining M&A deals in identity security — and by the end of this course, you'll be able to dissect it completely: the strategic rationale, the valuation methodology, the deal structure, the integration plan, and what Okta paid too much for. Whether you're planning to sell your startup or buy one, this course gives you the frameworks and financial tools to navigate M&A from both sides of the table.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish corporate strategy from business unit strategy; build a portfolio strategy using BCG and GE-McKinsey matrices
  • Evaluate M&A deals from strategic, financial, and operational perspectives
  • Model M&A valuation: DCF, trading comps, precedent transactions, and accretion/dilution analysis
  • Structure and negotiate M&A deals, including LOIs, term sheets, and closing conditions
  • Design post-merger integration frameworks that actually preserve deal value
