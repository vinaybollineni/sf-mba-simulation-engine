CAP 403 — Year 1 Capstone: Business Plan Sprint

Quarter 4 · Year 1 · 4 Weeks · Jun 14 – Jul 10, 2027

CAP 403

Year 1 Capstone: Business Plan Sprint

Upcoming — Quarter 4 · Year 1
View Curriculum →
Professor's Welcome Note:

This is it. Everything from ACCT 101 through ENT 402 comes down to this: can you build a real business plan? Not a school exercise — a document that could walk into a room with investors and hold up under scrutiny. You've spent a year learning to speak finance, strategy, marketing, operations, and entrepreneurship. Now you synthesize it. Four weeks. One business plan. This is your Year 1 graduation project.

Learning Objectives

  • Synthesize all Year 1 frameworks — finance, marketing, strategy, operations, and entrepreneurship — into one coherent business plan
  • Demonstrate MBA-level business thinking across all domains
  • Build a fundable business concept with a defensible market, realistic financial model, and compelling story
  • Present your business plan as a professional investor pitch

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 — Start Here
Executive Summary & Problem/Solution
Open →
Topics:
  • Define the problem and your unique insight
  • Articulate the solution and why now
  • Team narrative and founder-market fit
  • Write the one-pager that anchors the entire plan
Deliverables:
  • Executive summary draft (due Fri Jun 18)
  • Problem validation evidence — customer quotes, market data (due Sun Jun 20)
Week 2
Market Analysis, Strategy & Go-to-Market
Topics:
  • Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) with primary evidence
  • Competitive landscape (5-competitor matrix)
  • Porter's Five Forces for your market
  • Go-to-market strategy (channels/sales motion/initial customers)
Deliverables:
  • Market analysis section complete (due Fri Jun 25)
  • Competitive landscape matrix
  • GTM strategy outline
Week 3
Financial Model — 3-Year Projections
Topics:
  • Revenue model and pricing assumptions
  • Headcount plan
  • Monthly P&L model (12 months)
  • 3-year annual projections, break-even analysis, funding requirements and use of proceeds
Deliverables:
  • Full 3-year financial model (due Fri Jul 2)
  • Model assumptions document
  • Break-even chart
Week 4 · CAPSTONE DUE
Full Business Plan Presentation
Topics:
  • Complete 30-40 page business plan + 15-slide investor deck + 10-minute presentation
  • Present as if pitching to real investors
Deliverables:
  • Final: Complete business plan + investor deck + 10-minute presentation (due July 10, 2027)
  • Business plan document
  • Pitch recording
