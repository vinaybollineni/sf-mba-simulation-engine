MKT 502 — Growth Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Quarter 5 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Aug 4 – Oct 7, 2027

MKT 502

Growth Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Upcoming — Quarter 5
Go to Dashboard →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Most engineers think "build it and they will come." MKT 502 is the antidote. The best SaaS growth machines — Slack, Figma, Notion, Dropbox — aren't successful because of their code. They're successful because of their growth loops. This course teaches you to think like a head of growth: which channel will dominate, what's the viral coefficient, what's the payback period, and how do you know when to double down vs. kill a channel. By Week 10, you'll have a complete growth plan for your startup.

Learning Objectives

  • Build a full-funnel growth strategy: acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral (AARRR)
  • Design viral loops and referral programs with measurable k-factors
  • Apply Product-Led Growth (PLG) frameworks to B2B SaaS
  • Run and optimize paid acquisition across SEM, LinkedIn, and Meta
  • Define and move North Star metrics that actually predict long-term revenue

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Active Now
Growth Fundamentals — AARRR, Pirate Metrics & Funnel Strategy
Open →
Topics:
  • AARRR (Pirate Metrics) framework
  • Acquisition vs. retention trade-offs
  • Okta's growth vs. Dropbox PLG
  • Funnel modeling basics
Deliverables:
  • Map Okta's growth funnel (due Fri Aug 8)
  • Define your startup's AARRR metrics (due Sun Aug 10)
Week 2
PLG — Product-Led Growth for SaaS B2B
Locked
Freemium vs. free trial vs. reverse trial; Slack's viral growth mechanics; how Figma beat Adobe without enterprise sales; PLG applicability for identity and security products. When PLG works and when enterprise sales still wins.
Week 3
Content Marketing & SEO for B2B
Locked
Keyword strategy for enterprise SaaS; pillar + cluster content architecture; Okta's blog as a compounding growth asset; thought leadership as top-of-funnel demand generation. Building authority in a security category.
Week 4
Paid Acquisition — SEM, LinkedIn & Meta
Locked
Google search intent for security buyers; LinkedIn ABM (Account-Based Marketing); paid social for enterprise audiences; CPL/CPA benchmarks for identity SaaS. How to build attribution models when the sales cycle is 6+ months.
Week 5 · Mid-Project Due
Viral Loops & Referral Programs
Locked
Viral coefficient math; Dropbox referral case study; B2B referral vs. partner programs; building a referral mechanic into a security product. Mid-project due: Full acquisition strategy document for your startup concept.
Week 6
Email Marketing & Lifecycle Automation
Locked
Onboarding sequences; behavioral triggers; Intercom vs. HubSpot vs. Customer.io tradeoffs; how to reduce time-to-value with targeted email nudges. Designing lifecycle programs that convert trials into paying enterprise customers.
Week 7
Community-Led Growth & Developer Relations
Locked
Slack communities, Discord servers, developer relations as growth; how Auth0 built a developer community before Okta acquired them; the content → community → product flywheel. Why developer trust is the hardest and most durable moat.
Week 8
Growth Analytics & North Star Metrics
Locked
What makes a good North Star metric; how to instrument your product for growth analytics; Amplitude/Mixpanel dashboards; leading vs. lagging indicators. Case: How Slack chose "messages sent" and what that tells you about metric selection.
Week 9
International Expansion Strategy
Locked
Localization vs. translation; how Okta scaled from US-only to 17,000+ global customers; channel differences by geography; regulatory compliance as a growth constraint in EMEA and APAC. When to go international and how to resource it.
Week 10 · FINAL PROJECT DUE
Full Growth Plan for Your Startup
Locked
Final Project: Complete growth plan document covering: North Star metric, acquisition channel strategy, full funnel model, 12-month growth projections, and budget allocation by channel with expected ROI. Due: Oct 7, 2027.
