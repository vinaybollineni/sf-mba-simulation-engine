MKT 502 — Growth Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Quarter 5 · Year 2 · 10 Weeks · Aug 4 – Oct 7, 2027

MKT 502

Growth Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Upcoming — Quarter 5
Professor's Welcome Note:

Most engineers think "build it and they will come." MKT 502 is the antidote. The best SaaS growth machines — Slack, Figma, Notion, Dropbox — aren't successful because of their code. They're successful because of their growth loops. This course teaches you to think like a head of growth: which channel will dominate, what's the viral coefficient, what's the payback period, and how do you know when to double down vs. kill a channel. By Week 10, you'll have a complete growth plan for your startup.

Learning Objectives

  • Build a full-funnel growth strategy: acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral (AARRR)
  • Design viral loops and referral programs with measurable k-factors
  • Apply Product-Led Growth (PLG) frameworks to B2B SaaS
  • Run and optimize paid acquisition across SEM, LinkedIn, and Meta
  • Define and move North Star metrics that actually predict long-term revenue
