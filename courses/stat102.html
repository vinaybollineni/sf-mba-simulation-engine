STAT 102 — Business Statistics & Data-Driven Decisions

Quarter 1 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · Jun 2 – Jul 24, 2026

STAT 102

Business Statistics & Data-Driven Decisions

Upcoming — Starts Jun 2, 2026
Go to Week 1 →
Professor's Welcome Note:

You already work with data every day as an engineering manager — but there's a difference between raw data and statistical inference. Business statistics teaches you to design experiments, not just observe them. After this course, you'll be able to design an A/B test that will actually hold up to scrutiny, build regression models that predict business outcomes, and — critically — know when not to trust a model. For someone in your role at Okta, this is the bridge from technical intuition to evidence-based decision-making.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply statistical inference and hypothesis testing to business decisions
  • Design rigorous A/B tests for product and marketing experiments
  • Build regression models to forecast customer behavior and business outcomes
  • Communicate statistical findings clearly to non-technical executive audiences
  • Use simulation and scenario analysis to quantify business risk

Weekly Syllabus

Week 1 · Starts Jun 2
Descriptive Statistics & Business Metrics
Open →
Topics:
  • Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, percentiles
  • Business metrics: conversion rates, churn rate, ARPU, NRR
  • How to describe a dataset honestly
  • What engineers typically get wrong when summarizing data
Deliverables:
  • Problem Set 1 (due Jun 6)
  • Okta Metrics Analysis — pull 5 metrics from Okta's FY2025 10-K and describe their distributions
Week 2
Probability & Expected Value in Decision-Making
Locked
Probability fundamentals, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem. Expected value in business: pricing decisions, risk analysis, feature prioritization under uncertainty. Decision trees for go/no-go decisions. Deliverables: Problem Set 2, Decision tree for a product launch scenario.
Week 3
Hypothesis Testing — A/B Testing for Product Decisions
Locked
Null hypothesis, p-values, statistical significance vs. practical significance. Type I and Type II errors. A/B test design: sample size, power, duration. Why most A/B tests at tech companies are run incorrectly. Deliverables: Problem Set 3, Design a valid A/B test for Okta's login flow (or any product feature).
Week 4
Regression Analysis — Predicting Customer Behavior
Locked
Simple linear regression, multiple regression, interpretation of coefficients, R-squared, residual analysis. Business applications: predicting churn, forecasting revenue, identifying drivers of NPS. Deliverables: Problem Set 4, Build a regression model predicting SaaS customer churn (provided dataset).
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
Correlation vs. Causation in Business
Locked
Correlation coefficient, spurious correlations, confounding variables, Simpson's Paradox. Causal inference basics: natural experiments, difference-in-differences. Why correlation alone leads to bad business decisions. Deliverables: Problem Set 5, Mid-course project: Analyze a real correlation in Okta's metrics and determine if it's causal.
Week 6
Forecasting Models — Time Series & Trend Analysis
Locked
Time series components (trend, seasonality, cycles, noise), moving averages, exponential smoothing, ARIMA basics. Business forecasting: revenue models, capacity planning, demand forecasting for engineering resources. Deliverables: Problem Set 6, Build a 4-quarter revenue forecast for a SaaS company.
Week 7
Simulation, Scenario Analysis & Monte Carlo Methods
Locked
Monte Carlo simulation basics, probability distributions for business inputs, sensitivity analysis, scenario modeling. How CFOs and VCs use Monte Carlo to stress-test financial projections. Why point estimates are dangerous. Deliverables: Problem Set 7, Monte Carlo model: simulate 3-year revenue outcomes for a startup with uncertain growth rate.
Week 8 · FINAL CASE REPORT DUE
Data-Driven Marketing Strategy for a SaaS Product
Locked
Final Project: Integration of all course concepts. Real case analysis using publicly available data. Full data analysis report — analyze a real SaaS company's publicly available metrics, identify statistical patterns, design 2 experiments to test hypotheses, present findings in an executive memo (3 pages). Due Jul 24, 2026.
