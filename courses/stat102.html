STAT 102 — Business Statistics & Data-Driven Decisions

Quarter 1 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · Jun 2 – Jul 24, 2026

STAT 102

Business Statistics & Data-Driven Decisions

Upcoming — Starts Jun 2, 2026
Go to Week 1 →
Professor's Welcome Note:

You already work with data every day as an engineering manager — but there's a difference between raw data and statistical inference. Business statistics teaches you to design experiments, not just observe them. After this course, you'll be able to design an A/B test that will actually hold up to scrutiny, build regression models that predict business outcomes, and — critically — know when not to trust a model. For someone in your role at Okta, this is the bridge from technical intuition to evidence-based decision-making.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply statistical inference and hypothesis testing to business decisions
  • Design rigorous A/B tests for product and marketing experiments
  • Build regression models to forecast customer behavior and business outcomes
  • Communicate statistical findings clearly to non-technical executive audiences
  • Use simulation and scenario analysis to quantify business risk
