ETH 303 — Business Ethics, Leadership & Corporate Governance

Quarter 3 · Year 1 · 8 Weeks · Feb 9 – Apr 2, 2027

ETH 303

Business Ethics, Leadership & Corporate Governance

Upcoming — Starts Feb 9, 2027
Go to Week 1 →
Professor's Welcome Note:

Ethics isn't compliance — it's judgment. As you move toward VP and CTO roles, you'll face situations where the law says one thing and your values say another. Identity and security is one of the most ethically loaded domains in tech: you're building the systems that decide who gets access to what, for billions of people. This course will give you the frameworks to make hard calls under pressure — and to build organizations that don't need you in the room to do the right thing.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply ethical frameworks (utilitarian, deontological, virtue ethics) to real business dilemmas
  • Understand corporate governance structures: board composition, fiduciary duty, audit committees
  • Navigate stakeholder conflicts and growing ESG pressures on tech companies
  • Analyze ethics in tech: data privacy, AI bias, surveillance capitalism, responsible AI deployment
  • Build an ethical leadership framework for founding or leading a company

Topics:
