PM 801 — Platform Business Models & AI Strategy

Quarter 8 · Year 2 · 8 Weeks · May 5 – Jun 26, 2028

Professor's Welcome Note:

Your final seminar before the capstone. Okta is a platform — and you've been living inside one for years. PM 801 is about going meta: understanding why platforms win, how AI is rewriting the rules of platform competition, and how to design the next generation of AI-native business models. The identity layer sits at the intersection of every platform interaction — you are uniquely positioned to see what others miss. Use it.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish platform from pipeline businesses and explain why platforms generate superior returns
  • Analyze network effects: types, strength, defensibility, and when they reverse
  • Design a platform launch strategy that solves the cold start problem
  • Build an AI business model strategy: API economy, foundation models, and vertical AI plays
  • Apply data moat and identity layer strategies to competitive advantage

Week 2
Network Effects — Types, Strength, Defensibility
Locked
Direct vs. indirect; local vs. global; same-side vs. cross-side; how Okta's identity graph creates a direct network effect among IT admins; when network effects reverse (Friendster, MySpace).
Week 3
Platform Launch Strategy — Solving the Cold Start Problem
Locked
Andrew Chen's cold start theory; chicken-and-egg problems; seeding one side first; how Stripe, Uber, and Airbnb solved cold start; how Auth0 built a developer community from zero.
Week 4
Platform Monetization
Locked
Take rate strategies; freemium → enterprise conversion; per-seat vs. usage-based; marketplace fee structures; how Okta monetizes its Integration Network.
Week 5 · Mid-Course Project Due
AI Business Models — API Economy, Foundation Models
Locked
OpenAI's API model; vertical AI (Harvey, Jasper, Glean); AI infrastructure plays (Databricks, Anyscale); how identity and access management intersects with AI agent authorization. Mid-project: AI strategy for a platform business.
Week 6
Data Moats & Personalization at Scale
Locked
First-party data advantages; how Okta's login data creates intelligence no competitor can replicate; privacy-preserving personalization; federated learning.
Week 7
Regulatory Risk for Platforms
Locked
EU AI Act; GDPR platform implications; Section 230; antitrust pressure on big tech platforms; how regulatory moats can become regulatory burdens.
