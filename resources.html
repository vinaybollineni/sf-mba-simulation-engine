📊 Finance & Accounting

ACCT 101 · Wharton MBA Accounting

Financial & Managerial Accounting for MBAs — Dyckman, Magee & Pfeiffer

Wharton's actual MBA accounting textbook. Graduate-level treatment: focuses on analysis and judgment, not bookkeeping mechanics. Used in Wharton's ACCT 6110.

Amazon (~$50–80 used) ↗
📚 Buy or borrow from your library
FIN 201 · Used at Wharton, MIT, Columbia

Principles of Corporate Finance — Brealey, Myers & Allen

The gold standard corporate finance text. Used at virtually every top MBA program. DCF, WACC, capital structure, dividends, M&A. Chapters directly map to FIN 201.

📥 Free — Internet Archive
FIN 702 / ENT 501 · Standard in VC/Startup Finance

Venture Deals — Brad Feld & Jason Mendelson

Required reading in Stanford, Wharton, and Harvard entrepreneurship finance courses. Term sheets, cap tables, liquidation preferences — the language of startup finance.

📥 Free — Internet Archive
FIN 702 · Stanford Entrepreneurship Core Reading

The Hard Thing About Hard Things — Ben Horowitz

Commonly assigned at Stanford GSB entrepreneurship courses. The unvarnished reality of building a company — layoffs, down rounds, hard calls. Pairs with Venture Deals.

📥 Free — Internet Archive

♟️ Strategy

STR 301 · Harvard / Wharton / Stanford Core

Competitive Strategy — Michael Porter

Assigned at every top MBA strategy course worldwide. Five Forces, generic competitive strategies, value chain. The conceptual foundation of all MBA strategy teaching.

📥 Free — Internet Archive
STR 301 · Used at Kellogg, Tuck, Darden

Good Strategy Bad Strategy — Richard Rumelt

The clearest modern framework for diagnosing strategy from noise. "Kernel" model: diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions. Frequently assigned in MBA strategy courses.

📚 Buy or borrow from your library
PM 801 · MIT Sloan Platform Strategy

Platform Revolution — Parker, Van Alstyne & Choudary

The definitive MBA text on platform business models. Assigned in MIT Sloan's platform strategy courses. Directly maps to Okta's business model.

📥 Free — Internet Archive

⚙️ Operations Management

OPS 203 · Wharton MBA Operations Text

Matching Supply with Demand — Cachon & Terwiesch

Wharton's actual MBA operations management textbook. Replaces the undergraduate Heizer text. Process analysis, queuing theory, inventory management, newsvendor model. Written for MBA decision-makers.

📥 Free — Internet Archive
