Resources for ACCT 101 — Financial Accounting & Analysis (Weeks 1–10)

📖 Primary Textbook

Textbook

Financial Accounting, 5th Edition

Horngren, Sundem & Elliott — The standard accounting textbook used at Stanford GSB and Wharton. Clear, practical, and grounded in real company examples.

Chapters 1-2 due Week 1

🎥 Video Resources (Free)

Video Series

Khan Academy — Accounting & Financial Statements

Excellent free series covering all fundamentals. Start with "Accounting and Financial Statements" series. Very clear explanations for non-accountants.

Video Course

MIT OpenCourseWare — Financial Accounting (15.515)

Free MIT Sloan course materials. Excellent lecture notes and problem sets that supplement the textbook.

YouTube

Accounting Stuff (YouTube Channel)

Best YouTube channel for accounting fundamentals. Short, clear videos on every concept in this course. Highly recommended as a complement to reading.

🏢 Case Studies

Live Case · Week 1

Okta Inc. — Annual Report (10-K)

Download from SEC EDGAR. Find Okta's most recent 10-K. Your Week 1 case uses this — identify all balance sheet line items and explain each one.

🔗 sec.gov → Search "Okta" → 10-K
HBS Case · Week 5

Netflix Inc. — Financial Statement Analysis

Analyze Netflix's transition from DVD to streaming from a financial accounting perspective. Focus on how deferred revenue, content assets, and subscriber metrics changed.

Live Case · Week 10

Okta Inc. — Full Financial Analysis (Final)

Your final project: comprehensive analysis of all three financial statements, 5 years of trend data, ratio analysis, and an executive summary memo.

🏢 Technology & SaaS Cases (Tailored to Your Background)

ACCT 101 · Finance

Okta Inc. — Annual Report Analysis

Your primary live case. Use Okta's real 10-K throughout ACCT 101 to understand SaaS-specific accounting: deferred revenue, stock-based comp, RPO.

TEC 401 · Strategy

Okta's Platform Strategy — From IAM to Identity Cloud

How Okta evolved from single-product to platform. Analyze network effects, ecosystem strategy, and the Auth0 acquisition.

STR 301 · Strategy

Apple — Platform & Ecosystem Strategy

HBS Case. The gold standard for platform strategy and ecosystem control. Apply to understand what Okta could emulate.

OB 103 · Leadership

Satya Nadella — Microsoft's Cultural Transformation

How Nadella transformed Microsoft from a "know-it-all" to "learn-it-all" culture. Directly applicable to your leadership development path.

FIN 201 · Finance

Uber's Pre-IPO Valuation — HBS Case

Walk through the valuation of a high-growth, pre-profit tech company. Core skill for evaluating your own startup.

ETH 303 · Ethics

Theranos — The Cost of Fraud in Tech

What happens when a tech founder's narrative disconnects from reality. Critical case for founders on ethics, governance, and board dynamics.
